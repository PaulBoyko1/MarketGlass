# Quantitative Claims Register

| Concept | Classification | What MarketGlass says | What it does not say |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Advance/decline and percent positive | DEFINITION / DESCRIPTIVE RELATIONSHIP | Participation can differ from cap-weighted index movement | Breadth weakness predicts reversal |
| SPY versus RSP | DESCRIPTIVE RELATIONSHIP | Relative movement is a concentration/participation lens | A falling ratio is bearish |
| Realized volatility | DEFINITION | Annualized dispersion of a stated return interval and window | It is comparable to every implied-volatility horizon |
| VIX / IV | DEFINITION | An option-implied volatility context with stated horizon/model | A fear or directional signal |
| IV surface / skew | DEFINITION / DESCRIPTIVE RELATIONSHIP | Market quotes or a synthetic fixture vary by strike and maturity | A skew shape identifies an imminent move |
| Gamma exposure | DISPUTED / ASSUMPTION-SENSITIVE | OI plus Greeks produces an unsigned magnitude and alternative sign scenarios | Dealer inventory/hedging is observed from OI |
| Analogue distance | OUR ORIGINAL TEST | A transparent fixture-distance ranking and subsequent distribution | A forecast or causal match |
| Narrow-rally template | OUR ORIGINAL TEST | A synthetic chronological experiment with a holdout and multiple-testing warning | Published predictive evidence or a tradable signal |
| IV versus realized template | PUBLISHED PREDICTIVE EVIDENCE / OUR ORIGINAL TEST | A research template whose demo result is synthetic | A guaranteed volatility-risk-premium trade |

Demo market-state and options views carry synthetic provenance, while experiment exports carry the data hash and source label. The UI never converts these measures into a recommendation.
